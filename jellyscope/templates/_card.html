The Overview used to have its wrappers by hand and the custom dashboard none, so "Now playing" stood still there and the Movies/Series switch loaded the whole page. Expects `card` (an entry of the registry) and the card's data in the context (cards.data_for). The live wrapper (see cards_base.Card): * `refresh_partial` - the slice is fetched again in the collector's rhythm (data-refresh, data-partial; handled in static/js/app.js). Cards of the apps (cards_.py): * `d` - the card's data. They come under the card's key, so cards of several apps in one dashboard do not overwrite each other's variables. The card's template reads only `d.…`. * `prefix` - "Sonarr · " before the card's heading in a dashboard, where cards of several apps can stand side by side. On the app's own page (`in_dashboard` is not set) it is empty - it is clear there whose card it is. Older cards read neither.
